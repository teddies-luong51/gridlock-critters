# Market Research — Hyper-Casual & Hybrid-Casual Mobile (2024–2025)

*Prepared by product-researcher. Date: 2026-06-09. Sources: WebSearch (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, Appfigures, PocketGamer.biz, Gamigion, Deconstructor of Fun, Statista) + analyst knowledge to cutoff. Findings labeled **[Proven]** (market evidence) or **[Opportunity]** (gap/inference).*

---

## Step 1: Market Landscape

### Hyper-Casual (pure)
| Dimension | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Market size & growth | **[Proven]** Pure hypercasual revenue has *stalled/declined* as a standalone category. CPMs and IAA-only economics no longer cover rising UA costs; the category is being cannibalized by hybrid-casual. The "hyper-to-hybrid transition" is the defining structural move of 2024–2026. |
| Genre breakdown | Runners, .io merges, idle ASMR, ragdoll/physics, simple arcade. Increasingly used as a *UA hook funnel*, not an end product. |
| Platform split | Android-skewed for installs (cheap UA, IAA volume); iOS for higher per-user value. |
| Monetization mix | Almost pure IAA (interstitial + rewarded). This is the model that broke — too thin to survive 2024 CPM/UA economics. |
| Top publishers | Voodoo, Rollic (Zynga/Take-Two), SayGames, Supersonic (Unity), Homa, CrazyLabs — all of whom have **pivoted toward hybrid**. |

### Hybrid-Casual (the growth engine)
| Dimension | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Market size & growth | **[Proven]** Hybrid-casual IAP revenue grew **~67% YoY in Q1 2025**, accelerating to **~100% YoY in Q2 2025**. Top-10 hybrid-casual titles generated **$126M in a single quarter (Q2 2025)**; hybrid-casual IAP exceeded **$345M in H1 2025**. Q3 2025 showed **114% YoY** growth. Standalone IAP for the segment rose ~20–37% YoY to ~$4.2B. |
| Genre breakdown | **Puzzle dominates** — over half of top-10 revenue is puzzle; arcade ~35%. Block puzzle, screw/sort puzzle, merge, idle-RPG/roguelite mash-ups. |
| Platform split | Dual-monetization (IAA + IAP) means iOS and Android both matter; iOS for IAP whales, Android for ad-funded scale. |
| Monetization mix | **[Proven]** Hybrid = IAA + IAP together. Signature tactic: **"fail offers"** triggered after a level loss (Screwdom's "keep up" offer = ~14.5% of total IAP, ~17% of first purchases). Plus boosters, session-extension bypasses, light seasonal events/passes. |
| Top publishers | Rollic (4 of top-10 in Q3 2025), Voodoo, HABBY (idle-RPG hybrid powerhouse, $1bn+ lifetime), Playrix (casual meta-builders), Supercell-adjacent merge studios. |
| Regional growth | North America +9%, Europe +14%, LATAM +13%, Middle East +18%, Oceania +8% YoY IAP. Western markets lead absolute IAP; Asia (KR/TW) over-indexes on idle-RPG spend. |

**Headline:** Pure hypercasual is **Declining as a business model**; hybrid-casual is the **fastest-growing casual segment**, led by puzzle. The winning pattern is a hypercasual *hook* re-engineered with a meta layer + dual monetization.

## Step 2: Top Titles Deep Dive

Three benchmarks across distinct sub-genres: **block/spatial puzzle**, **idle-RPG/roguelite mash-up**, **casual meta-builder (match-3)**.

### Color Block Jam — Rollic (Zynga) — Block/Spatial Puzzle (hybrid-casual)
- **Downloads / Revenue tier:** **[Proven]** 30M+ downloads; **$25M in Q1 2025** (up from just $175K in Q4 2024), **$42M in Q2 2025**. Explosive vertical takeoff.
- **Core loop (30-sec):** Drag colored blocks across a tight grid → push each block out through its matching-color gate → board clears → next, harder layout. Pure spatial-reasoning satisfaction.
- **Meta layer:** Light — level progression, increasing board complexity, daily/limited events. Meta is thin by design; depth comes from puzzle difficulty, not a big economy.
- **Monetization:** IAA (interstitial + rewarded) layered with high-converting IAP — booster packs, extra moves, and **fail offers** after a loss. Aggressive mass-market UA paired with this dual model.
- **Visual style:** Clean, bright, high-contrast flat shapes on a minimal board — instantly readable in a 15-sec ad.
- **Retention signals:** Strong early retention typical of block-puzzle (clear D1; meta-light so D7+ relies on difficulty pacing + events).
- **Does exceptionally well:** Mash-up of Block Puzzle + Parking Jam = *novel but instantly legible* core. Perfect ad-creative legibility drove cheap UA. Fail-offer monetization captures intent at the exact frustration peak.
- **Does poorly / complaints:** Thin meta = D30 fall-off risk; difficulty spikes and forced-ad frustration cited in reviews; long-term depth questioned.
- **Differentiable gap:** Beat it with a *deeper meta/collection layer* on top of an equally legible spatial core, or a fresh spatial twist (rotation, gravity, layered boards) that ads can still show in 5 seconds.

### Capybara Go! — HABBY — Text-based Roguelite + Idle RPG (mash-up)
- **Downloads / Revenue tier:** **[Proven]** Crossed **$100M gross player spend within ~3 months** of global launch (Nov 2024 → Feb 2025); peaked **$33.3M/month (Dec 2024)**, settling to ~$19M/month by mid-2025.
- **Core loop (30-sec):** Auto-battle/idle combat punctuated by **randomized roguelite event choices** (branching text encounters) → pick skills, gear, animal allies → power up → push further into a run. Decision-driven, low-input.
- **Meta layer:** Deep — gear, skill builds, companion collection, run-based progression with permanent upgrades. The roguelite *run* + collection drives strong session returns.
- **Monetization:** IAP-heavy (gacha-adjacent gear/companion pulls, packs, battle-pass-style progression) + rewarded ads. Classic HABBY "hybridcasual empire" template: simple input, deep economy.
- **Visual style:** Cute capybara mascot + cozy charm wrapped around a surprisingly deep RPG — adorable surface, deep guts.
- **Retention signals:** Massive early monetization but **[Proven]** notable WAU/revenue decay through 2025 (weekly revenue $1M→$382K in Q2) — peaky lifecycle typical of HABBY hits. KR (28%) & TW (17%) over-index.
- **Does exceptionally well:** Charm-forward mascot lowers the barrier to a deep system; randomized events make idle feel *agentic*; HABBY's proven economy converts hard early.
- **Does poorly / complaints:** Steep retention decay; gacha/pay-pressure complaints; text-heavy core limits some Western mass-market reach.
- **Differentiable gap:** Beat it with **stronger long-tail retention** (live-ops cadence, social/guild layer) and a less pay-aggressive curve, or the same charm+depth in a more visual (less text) core for Western audiences.

### Royal Match — Dream Games — Match-3 + Meta-Builder (casual)
- **Downloads / Revenue tier:** **[Proven]** **Top-grossing casual game worldwide, June 2025 — $117M monthly IAP** (ahead of Monopoly Go! at $103M). Sustained top-grossing leader.
- **Core loop (30-sec):** Swap-and-match-3 board with satisfying combo cascades and special-piece chains → clear objectives → win moves toward a goal.
- **Meta layer:** Renovate/decorate the King's castle (the Playrix "Scapes" meta formula, executed cleaner) — strong narrative-light decoration goal that pulls players through match levels. Heavy live-ops, team/social events.
- **Monetization:** IAP-dominant — coins, lives, boosters, move packs; deep, well-tuned difficulty gating that nudges purchase at fail points. Minimal forced ads (premium-feel).
- **Visual style:** Polished, warm, premium 3D castle/character art — "AAA casual" production value.
- **Retention signals:** Best-in-class long-term retention via relentless live-ops, events, and decoration goal loop; the genre's gold standard for D30+.
- **Does exceptionally well:** Cleanest execution of match-3 + meta; difficulty tuning + booster economy maximize LTV without feeling hostile; live-ops machine.
- **Does poorly / complaints:** Difficulty-wall/pay-to-progress complaints at higher levels; genre is mature and *very* expensive to compete in on UA.
- **Differentiable gap:** Nearly impossible to beat head-on (capital + live-ops moat). Beat *adjacent* — bring its meta-polish discipline to a **less saturated core** than match-3.

## Step 3: Trend Signals

| Signal | Status | Evidence |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Hybrid-casual (overall) | **Rising** | 67%→100%→114% YoY IAP growth across 2025 quarters; $345M+ IAP H1 2025. |
| Pure hypercasual (IAA-only business) | **Declining** | Stalled revenue; UA economics broke; studios pivoting to hybrid. Now a *funnel*, not a product. |
| Block / spatial puzzle (Color Block Jam type) | **Rising** | Color Block Jam $175K→$42M/quarter; puzzle = 50%+ of top-10 hybrid revenue. |
| Screw / sort / "tidy" puzzle (Screwdom type) | **Rising** | Screwdom $27.1M Q2 2025; fail-offer monetization proven. |
| Idle-RPG / roguelite mash-ups (HABBY template) | **Rising (peaky)** | Capybara Go $100M in 3 months; but steep retention decay = high-burn lifecycle. |
| Match-3 + meta-builder | **Stable (mature, dominant)** | Royal Match $117M/mo; category saturated, huge incumbents, expensive UA. Showing modest *revival* signs after years of saturation. |
| Merge games (Travel Town type) | **Stable** | Core hybrid pillar; proven but crowded. |
| Tower-defense × roguelite hybrids | **Rising (emerging)** | Multiple 2025 launches (Auto Defense, Everwarder, Lone Tower); TD being merged with roguelite/idle action — early but momentum building. |
| Endless runner (standalone) | **Declining** | Survives mainly as a UA hook or with bolted-on meta economy. |
| Dual monetization (IAA + IAP together) | **Rising → new standard** | Now the default winning model for hybrid; not optional. |
| **Fail offers** (post-loss IAP triggers) | **Rising** | Screwdom "keep up" = ~14.5% of IAP, ~17% of first purchases; now a category-wide best practice. |
| Cozy/cute mascot framing over deep systems | **Rising** | Capybara Go proves charm lowers the barrier to depth; capybara/animal-cozy theme has cultural momentum. |
| AAA-casual production value | **Rising** | Royal Match / Dream Games raised the visual-polish bar; "good enough" art no longer wins casual. |

**Emerging mechanics (<12 months):** TD×roguelite hybrids; text/event-choice roguelite layered on idle; spatial-puzzle mash-ups (block+jam, screw+3D). **Themes with momentum:** cozy animals (capybara), satisfying-organization/ASMR ("tidy" puzzles), light roguelite agency in idle games.

## Step 4: Best Practices (synthesized from the benchmarks)

**Core Loop**
- Session sweet spot 2–5 min; fail-and-restart under 3 seconds (Color Block Jam restart speed is a UA asset).
- One new mechanic per ~5 levels max; tutorial *is* play (drag the first block to win, never a text wall).
- **The core must be ad-legible in 5 seconds** — Color Block Jam's takeoff was driven by instantly-readable creative. If you can't show the loop in a 15-sec ad, UA dies.

**Meta Layer**
- First upgrade/reward inside the first 3 minutes.
- Add a meta layer to the hook — a meta-light hit (Color Block Jam) risks D30 decay; a meta-rich hit (Capybara Go) monetizes hard but can over-burn. Aim between: a *collection* + *light decoration/build goal* + *event cadence*.
- Collection mechanics (companions, themes, decorations) materially lift D7+; streaks add ~15–30% D3 lift.
- Royal Match's decoration goal proves a *non-gameplay aspiration* pulls players through the gameplay grind.

**Monetization**
- **Dual model is mandatory** (IAA + IAP). Pure IAA is the model that died.
- **Implement fail offers** — post-loss "continue/keep-up" packs convert at the frustration peak (proven ~14–17% of IAP/first purchases).
- Rewarded video at "I want more" moments only; interstitial cap ~1 per 3 levels/min.
- Starter pack $0.99–$1.99 converts 3–5x better as first purchase than $4.99; ad-removal best at $2.99–$3.99.
- Premium-feel titles (Royal Match) keep forced ads low and lean IAP; ad-funded scale titles (Color Block Jam) run heavier IAA — pick your lane deliberately.

**Avoid**
- No progress paywalls in first 20 levels.
- No ads during active gameplay (immersion break = uninstall).
- Max 1 soft + 1 hard currency.
- Don't ship a meta-thin core into a long-LTV ambition — Color Block Jam's main risk.
- Don't out-spend Royal Match at its own game — match-3+meta is a capital/live-ops moat.
